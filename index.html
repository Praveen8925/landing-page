HYGIENE & EDUCATION

How Long to Use & Responsible Disposal

Simple, healthy habits for your wellbeing and environmental cleanliness.

⏰

How Long to Use?

Government of India (MoHFW) Guideline
Change your pad every 4–6 hours

To stay clean, fresh, confident, and prevent rashes or bacterial growth.

  • ☀️
    Day Time Routine

    Change pad every 4 to 6 hours depending on your flow.

  • 🌙
    Night Time Routine

    Change right before sleeping and immediately after waking up.

  • 💧
    Heavy Flow Days

    Change sooner whenever the pad feels full or damp.

♻️

How to Use & Dispose

Safe Application & Environment Care
How to Use:
  1. Peel off the release paper.
  2. Place the pad in the center of your underwear.
  3. Press gently to stick wings properly.
  4. Change as needed for hygiene.
Disposing Method:
  1. Remove the used pad.
  2. Wrap in paper or disposal cover.
  3. Throw inside dry dustbin.
  4. Do NOT flush in toilet.
